Cheese Cake Cups

Description

These Cheesecake Cups are a delightful, no-fuss dessert that delivers all the rich, creamy flavor of classic cheesecake in perfectly portioned servings. Each cup starts with a buttery, crumbly base—typically made from crushed biscuits or graham crackers—pressed firmly to create a satisfying crunch that contrasts beautifully with the smooth filling above.

The cheesecake layer is luxuriously creamy, made with cream cheese, a touch of sugar, and vanilla for balanced sweetness and depth of flavor. Chilled to set, these cups can be topped with fresh fruit, fruit compote, chocolate drizzle, or caramel, making them easy to customize for any occasion. Ideal for parties, gatherings, or make-ahead desserts, Cheesecake Cups are elegant, convenient, and irresistibly delicious

    Prep Time:
  • 15 mins
    • Cook Time:
  • 15 mins
    • Total Time:
  • 30 mins
    • Servings:
  • 16
    • Yield:
  • 16 cupcakes

    Ingredients

  • 16 vanilla wafer cookies
  • 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • ¾ cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

    Directions

    Step 1

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Line muffin pans with cupcake papers.

    2. Step 2

  2. Place one wafer cookie in the bottom of each cupcake paper. In a medium bowl, cream together cream cheese and sugar. Beat in eggs and vanilla until smooth. Pour over wafers in cupcake papers.

    3. Step 3

  3. Bake in preheated oven 15 minutes, until golden and set.
